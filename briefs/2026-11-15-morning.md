**Bahrain (fire confirmed; a hit is not).**
- **Friday, 13 Nov:** CENTCOM said: "The IRGC launched two one-way attack drones toward Bahrain and both were shot down by U.S. forces. There were no injuries or damage." The statement was out by 18:44 IST.
- **This morning:** sirens sounded at ~07:00 IST. At 07:43 IST, Bahrain's state television said air defences "intercepted and destroyed an Iranian drone".
  - **The Guards' claim (unconfirmed):** six drones hit "a radar at a US air-defence site" at the Al-Rifa'a helicopter base. They call it the "second stage" of their response to US "clear aggression". They name no new attack on Iran. CENTCOM had not commented by 10:10 IST.
- **Not a first:** in July Bahrain's foreign ministry condemned Iranian drone strikes on its air-traffic systems, and the Guards then claimed a Patriot battery at Riffa.

No source links the Riyadh and Bahrain salvos, which came about 20 minutes apart.
