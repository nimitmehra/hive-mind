## I. What Happened

### After Iranian drones targeted a US radar in Bahrain, the US struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm on Tuesday. Iran then fired at Bahrain again and at Kuwait, its first attack there since September (Reuters). The NYT reports Trump is weighing "another round of extensive military strikes"; no decision has been reported.
**The strike (confirmed; US ownership reported).**
- **Iran's side:** Hormozgan's deputy governor, Ahmad Nafisi, told IRNA: "The American attack on locations on Qeshm Island caused no damage to residential, commercial, or infrastructural areas."
- **The US side:** unnamed military officials told Reuters, Fox and CNN that US jets hit "an Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps missile launch site on Qeshm Island in self-defense". No US official has owned it on the record; we found no CENTCOM (US Central Command), Pentagon or White House statement on it.
- **What it was:** one launcher site by the US account (Iran's governorate speaks of "locations" on the island), hit in the Tehran afternoon or at dusk (first report found: the FT, 18:32 IST), like the strikes of 2–9 November; the first strike on Iranian territory reported since the night of 12–13 November.

Iran's army claims it hit "Patriot air defence systems" at Ali Al Salem air base (unconfirmed). Kuwait's foreign ministry called it "a dangerous escalation". But the rule's closing line, that single "defensive" counter-launcher hits on Qeshm "are logged, not fired", points the other way; the 7-Nov precedent fired leg (a) on an owned strike of this kind.

**The case for acting now:** three settles below $95 with the physical premium gone; thirteen sessions of foreign buying in a record month; the rupee at a seven-month high; the Nifty above its pre-war level; every Iranian drone at a US host intercepted.
