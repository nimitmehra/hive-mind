## I. What Happened

### Trump said "We don't need a deal with Iran." Oil rose 3.3% to $95.82. The same day the US ten-year yield rose to 5.10% on bets the Fed will keep raising rates, and India's inflation came in at 6.09%. On Thursday's figures, all three of our buying conditions fail.
**The post (confirmed: the words and the time).**
- **Trump on Truth Social, 18:37 IST Thursday** (08:07 New York time; we decoded the post's ID, and an archive gives the same minute): "We don't need a deal with Iran. Those days have long since passed… We've got their money, Hundreds of Billions of Dollars, and we've got their Strait!"
- **In the same post** he moved Vice-President Vance, who a day earlier was reported to be weighing direct talks with Iran, onto "corruption" in Democratic-run states.
- **What it is not:** an order. It announces no strike and does not say the talks are closed (NBC). It says the US is "locked and loaded" if things change (NBC). The White House would not say whether talks had ended: "President Trump holds all the cards." An unnamed official told Breitbart that if Trump "ever sees a smart deal worth looking at, he'll send the VP."
- **In two interviews** (reports differ on which line came from which), Trump said Saudi Arabia "asked me to please just let it be the way it is now for a while", which he gave as a reason for holding off. He also said a campaign "would only take me three days", and told Hugh Hewitt he would strike again "if I saw things going the other way", adding that he "might do it right now" (via the Times of Israel; Salem's own caption summarised it as "no hesitation"). No Saudi comment was found.

**Oil (confirmed price; the causes are as the wires give them).**
- **The post minute:** Brent traded 611 contracts that minute, against 3–26 a minute before it. It rose $2, from $92.09 to $94.10, in six minutes. S&P futures, Treasury futures and gold all fell. CNBC, Bloomberg and Reuters tie the jump to the post.
- **The evening:**
  - A dip at 22:30 IST, when the US Energy Information Administration reported crude stocks up 4.7 million barrels against a forecast fall.
  - Rises around the Houthis' claims on Saudi oil sites, which Saudi Arabia has not confirmed.
  - A climb into the settle. It overlaps the first reports of a strike in southern Iran (below); no outlet links the two.
- **The settle:** $95.82, up $3.06 (Reuters: "Oil settles $3 higher on attacks on Saudi energy sites, Strait of Hormuz disruptions"). It was the biggest daily rise since 5 November.
- **The shape:** the nearest contract rose most. The gap between January and December 2027 widened from $13.83 to $15.38, so the war premium sits at the front of the curve.
- **Today:** ~$95.5.

**US rates (confirmed; mostly the Fed, not the post).**
- **The closes (Treasury data):** the ten-year closed at **5.10%**, up 12 basis points, its biggest one-day rise since 23 September. The two-year rose 13bp to 4.86%. It was the first close since the Veterans Day holiday.
- **Why:** on our minute-by-minute data, the post's minutes account for about 30% of Thursday's fall in ten-year futures, and most of that reversed before the US open. About two-thirds of the fall came in US hours, as traders priced more Fed rate rises (CNBC: oil, Tuesday's inflation figure, and Goldman Sachs now forecasting a March rise).
  - **The San Francisco Fed's Daly:** "My bias is that further tightening will be needed."
  - **Not the cause:** the 30-year bond auction and weekly jobless claims barely moved bonds.
- **Credit is calm:** the junk-bond spread, the extra yield on risky company debt, was 3.30% on 11 November, narrower than before.

**India's inflation (confirmed).**
- **The figure:** October's consumer prices rose **6.09%** from a year earlier, up from 5.54% in September. That is above the Reserve Bank's 6% upper limit; Reuters calls it "the first time under the new series" (the 2024-based index).
- **The drivers:** food prices rose 8.94%, with onions up 116%. Electricity, gas and other fuels rose 3.71%, and transport 5.84%.
- **What economists now expect:** a rate rise on 4 December. ICRA calls it "almost certain", and Goldman Sachs sees three more quarter-point rises.
- **Bonds:** India's ten-year yield was 7.31% at 10:00 IST (one relay), up from ~7.25%.

### Over Iran, night ten was not quiet: Iranian state media say "the enemy" struck Sirik on the Hormuz coast. The Revolutionary Guards say they destroyed a US radar in Bahrain in reply.
- **Sirik (reported; Iranian state media only):** the official news agency IRNA, citing officials, said "Sirik in Hormozgan was struck by the enemy". It said the targets were military.
  - **Timing:** first reports at 00:15 IST; IRNA at about 00:40 IST.
  - **The other accounts:** Fars, the agency close to the Guards, first called the blasts warning shots at ships. The Guards say the target was a telecoms mast.
  - **Who did it:** no one has named the attacker on the record. The Guards' Bahrain statement treats the US as the aggressor. No US official has claimed or denied it, and US Central Command has said nothing.
  - **Not new as a target:** Sirik was among reported blast sites on 7–8 November, and the Guards said US drones hit a mast there in June.
  - **A second site, Bandar Lengeh,** rests on one social-media account.
- **Bahrain (claimed):** the Guards say they "targeted and destroyed" a US early-warning radar "on Bahrain island" (first reports 07:42 IST today; Reuters reports only that they say they targeted it).
  - **Silence:** Bahrain, the US and Central Command have not commented.
  - **Why it matters:** Reuters and the Times of Israel call it Iran's first claimed attack on US military assets in a Gulf state "since early September".
  - **The price:** Brent rose ~$0.7 in the 20 minutes after the claim, then gave most of it back.
- **Iran's other words:**
  - **The nuclear treaty:** the Assembly of Experts, the clerical body that chooses the Leader, called a review of Iran's membership of the nuclear Non-Proliferation Treaty (NPT) an "inevitable necessity".
    - **Not new in substance:** MPs and rallies demanded withdrawal on 29–30 October and 4–5 November. The government's line is still "at the decision-making level, we are still a member" (foreign ministry, 2 November). What is new is the rank of the body.
  - **President Pezeshkian:** Israel, "by deceiving Trump, has pushed him toward war".
  - **No reply to the post:** no named Iranian official had answered it by 11:10 IST.

### At sea, the UK navy's shipping office confirmed that the Bahri-managed *NCC Fajr* was attacked. A third tanker with Indian crew in two days, *Dominica Prosperity*, was hit off the UAE.
- ***NCC Fajr*** (Saudi-flagged; managed by a unit of Bahri, Saudi Arabia's national shipping company; three Indian crew): UKMTO reclassified it as an attack after confirming the ship was hit, north of Oman's Khasab, early on 11 November (gCaptain). No attacker has been named.
- ***Dominica Prosperity*** (UKMTO notice 204-26): hit in the engine room by an unidentified projectile, ~14 nautical miles north-west of Ras Al Khaimah, at ~23:30 IST on 11 November.
  - **The crew:** all 22, including 19 Indians, were taken off by the UAE coast guard.
  - **The manager:** a ship registry lists Sinokor of South Korea. Trade press says Sinokor also operates *Fujairah Energy*, hit the day before (a registry lists ADNOC's shipping arm).
  - **The blacklist:** Sinokor was the largest group on Iran's 2 November blacklist (at least 29 linked ships, as reported on 3 November). We do not know whether either ship is on it.
- **The count:** the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC), a naval information cell, logged four tanker attacks this week and 181 incidents since 1 March. It counts ~211 ships waiting at anchor east of the strait.
- **Iran's claims:** both of Iran's navies issued separate new claims that tankers hit mines. None named a ship.

### The Houthis claimed their most specific strikes yet on Saudi oil sites, by our reading. Saudi Arabia has confirmed none, and reported four drones shot down.
- **The claims (unconfirmed):** the Houthi military spokesman named "pumping station No. 7" on the East–West pipeline (in relays of his statement; Reuters says "a pumping station"), hit twice, the "Yanbu oil facility", "Aramco company in Riyadh", an air base and an airport. They came in four statements from 22:34 IST Thursday to 02:00 IST Friday. Reuters: Saudi Arabia "did not immediately confirm".
- **The Saudi-led coalition (reported; its own account only):** four drones were intercepted in two announcements, over the Riyadh region and the Eastern Province (Xinhua's total; Reuters and AA reported the first two). It reported no missiles and no damage.
- **The UN Security Council** renewed sanctions on the Houthis unanimously, to November 2027.
- **Brussels:** Reuters, citing six European officials, reports that Riyadh is pressing the EU to list the Houthis as terrorists.
- **Tadawul,** the Saudi exchange, rose 0.18% to a one-month high, with Aramco up 1.5%.

### Israel's brakes on a strike are reported to be holding. Its security cabinet meets on Sunday.
- **Channel 13 (unnamed sources):** at a two-hour briefing, the army told Netanyahu and the mandate-holder Gadi Eisenkot there is "no immediate urgency" to strike Iran, but that Israel should join if Trump renews US attacks.
- **Katz, in a Defence Ministry statement:** he, Netanyahu and army chief Zamir "see eye to eye on the Iranian issue in all its aspects".
- **The cabinet:** Netanyahu's office has announced the Sunday meeting (Al-Monitor). Its agenda, to "decide how to proceed on the Iranian front", rests on one outlet that names no source (JFeed). Ynet's Thursday story no longer says the cabinet "may now approve action".
- **Kan (one US source):** Central Command told Israeli army representatives to prepare for a possible resumption of fighting. Central Command: "mere speculation".

### Markets: gold had its lowest close of the year. Nvidia's chief executive resigned. European gas rose 6.5%, mostly on weather and Norway.
- **Gold** fell 2.9% to **$3,885**, its lowest close of 2026 and ~27% below January's closing high. Kitco attributes it to rising yields and a firm dollar; gold also fell in the minute of Trump's post.
- **Nvidia:** Jensen Huang resigned as chief executive and director (company filing, 19:22 IST). The shares fell 4.3%; the S&P 500 fell 0.35%. This is not a war story.
- **European gas** rose **6.5%** to €92.26. Most of the rise came before the post. Trading Economics cites cold forecasts, an outage at Norway's Troll field and low storage. We found no Qatari event.

### India: the rupee fell before the inflation figure came out. Foreign investors bought for an eighth day. Today, shares are slipping on rate-rise bets.
- **The rupee** closed at **94.04–94.12** on Thursday, from 93.71 (PTI; Reuters 94.04). It fell between noon and 15:30 IST, before the inflation release. Reuters cites oil, fading inflows and "a state-run lender" buying dollars.
- **Shares:** the Nifty closed at **25,299.35** (+0.18%), its second close above the pre-war level. Foreign investors bought ₹7,831 crore, their eighth straight session.
- **Today:** the Nifty was 25,191 (−0.4%) at 11:05 IST, after dipping below the pre-war level at 09:20, with banks leading the fall on rate-rise bets.
- **Fuel retailers** fell both days as Brent rose. Prime Minister Modi said the government is absorbing ₹14,000 crore a month on diesel (BusinessToday's paraphrase). Pump prices are unchanged.
- **New Delhi:** the shipping minister reported the *Dominica Prosperity* crew safe. The foreign ministry has said nothing since Tuesday.
