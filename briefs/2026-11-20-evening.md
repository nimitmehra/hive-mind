## I. What Happened

### Trump put his Iran decision past New Year in three interviews; on Friday CBS reported that the US and Israel are considering an assault "in the short term". Neither is an order, and night seventeen was quiet.
- **Trump (confirmed that he said it; a timeline, not a decision):** on Thursday, flying home from Shenzhen, he told Reuters, "Well, I'll talk to you about it after the beginning of the year" and "We've got time"; Axios and Channel 12, "I am not rushing into any decision. We will see what happens after the beginning of the year"; and Fox (in an interview aired Thursday), "We'll see what happens after January 1." He put a date on it, past the 30-Nov strike window. Axios notes he said he was undecided the day before February's war began.
- **Still on the table (reported):** Axios says a joint operation with Israel is still being weighed and "could include" suspected nuclear sites such as Pickaxe Mountain. CBS (Friday, 17:32 IST; one outlet, unnamed sources) says the two are "considering launching a new assault on Iran in the short term", which "could be mounted within two to four days, if such an order were given". The White House declined to comment.
- **Israel:** the IDF spokesman, Brig. Gen. Defrin, on the record: "no change to the current instructions" for the public (Home Front guidance), not a comment on plans. The leaks ran hotter (reported): Channel 12 (unsourced) says Israel "geared up several times" for US attacks that Trump then called off; Ynet's unnamed sources say "the die has been cast", without saying whose strike. No cabinet decision has been reported.
- **What did not happen:** no strike on Iranian territory reported on night seventeen; no new Iranian fire at a Gulf state hosting US forces found since Wednesday 18 November; no US official has owned the Qeshm strike on the record. The Senate rejected an Iran war-powers resolution, 48–51 (confirmed).

### Yemen: Reuters' sources say a Houthi missile hit Riyadh's PIF Tower, and on Friday the coalition said it struck Hodeidah port, the condition the Houthis set for attacking "all Saudi ports along the Red Sea coast". No port strike had followed by ~20:25 IST.
- **The tower (damage confirmed; cause reported):** the hit came on Thursday, and is probably the Riyadh "material damage" our last brief carried, now identified (our reading). Saudi Civil Defence reported "material damage to several floors" of a financial-district tower, with no injuries; the state news agency's photo shows the PIF Tower, home of the sovereign wealth fund. Three Reuters sources say a Houthi missile hit it; the coalition speaks of "debris". Riyadh reserves "all necessary measures".
- **Hodeidah (the coalition's own statements):** it told civilians to leave the port (by ~16:20 IST), then announced strikes on "operational capabilities at Hodeidah port" (19:02–19:28 IST, per relays). No independent damage report; Yemen's Houthi-run information ministry says the port is "fully operational".
- **The threat (rhetoric, its condition now met):** Houthi spokesman Saree named Jeddah and "all Saudi ports along the Red Sea coast", told shipping companies to "immediately evacuate their ships from these ports", and named the Shuqaiq power station.
- **Also:** Houthi claims to have hit the SADAF petrochemical complex in Jubail are unconfirmed. The commander of CENTCOM (US Central Command) met Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman in Riyadh (reported by the Saudi news agency early Thursday); no US strike on the Houthis followed. No Houthi attack on a non-Saudi ship for 70 days; nothing new from the Aden front.
