**Iran's night (reported).** No Iranian official or state outlet, no wire service, and no US or Israeli source reported a strike on Iranian territory on Sunday night. **Washington (rhetoric and posture).**
- **No response:** we found no US strike, no CENTCOM statement and no official calling Sunday's Iranian fire at Bahrain or the Houthi missiles at Riyadh "provocations". (CENTCOM, US Central Command, is the US command for the Middle East.) On Saturday, Reuters' sources said Trump "may hold off… if the US faces no new provocations".
- **The message was economic pressure:** Treasury Secretary Bessent told Fox: "We are squeezing them, and their death threats tell me that everything I'm doing is working." The State Department told Americans in Bahrain to "exercise heightened vigilance due to the increased threat of armed attacks".

- **The plans (reported; we read only relays of the NYT story):** citing unnamed officials, the NYT says the US and Israel "have drawn up plans for renewed strikes" likely aimed at "energy infrastructure and other facilities vital to its economy". This is not new in substance: the paper's 24-October story already listed "energy infrastructure and other facilities vital to Iran's economy" (in our 26-October brief), though some relays said refineries would be spared. What is new is the timing: the report follows Sunday's fire and comes as a third carrier arrives.

No end time, no statement from the prime minister's office or the army, and no leak of a decision appeared in any outlet we could read up to 12:25 IST. We could not open Kan, Haaretz or Israel Hayom. ### Yemen's government pulled its forces back from the Bab el-Mandeb coast to defend Aden. Reuters says that leaves the Houthis in control of the land beside the strait. No ship has been attacked.
- **The withdrawal (confirmed):** the pro-government Southern Armed Forces announced a "redeployment" from "Dhubab airport and the Bab Al Mandeb area". They said the reason was "the priority of securing the capital, Aden". Reuters, AFP and Al Jazeera all carried the statement.
- **Where the Houthis are (reported):** Reuters says the pullback leaves "the Iran-aligned Houthis in control" and "hands the Houthis greater leverage over the maritime choke point". AFP's government sources put Houthi forces at the Kharaz camp, "60 kilometres" from Aden. Government forces regrouped about 150 km west of Aden and are "still trying to push the Houthis" from nearby coast (Reuters).
- **What is new:** our record has Perim island and Mocha in Houthi hands since 10–11 September, and government forces taking the Dhubab airfield in early October (AFP, recorded 13 October). Sunday's withdrawal hands that gain back.
- **Shipping:** no Houthi attack on a ship that is not Saudi-linked for 66 days.

### The Signal You Might Miss
**If the US acts on the plans the NYT reports, one night of US strikes on energy targets would reset the hold rule on its own, with no three-night wait. It would also hit oil supply directly, which interceptions over Bahrain and claims about mines have not.**
- **The rule:** our reset has four legs. Leg (d) is "a strike on nuclear, energy or leadership targets". No strike reported since 4 November has met leg (c) or (d), so a reset has depended on US ownership (a) or three nights in a row (b). A US strike on an energy target would meet leg (d) in a single night. An Israeli strike alone would not reset it: that is your open decision (i).
- **The market:** on Monday Brent opened below Friday's settle despite the weekend's fire, on the surplus story (the IEA last week; Goldman today). A strike on Iranian energy facilities would hit that surplus directly, unlike interceptions over Bahrain or claims about mines. Condition 1 would then move further out of reach.
- **What we do not know:** whether the plans will be used. The report rests on unnamed officials, and we read only relays of it. Two waves of Iranian fire at Bahrain (Friday and Sunday) and Houthi missiles at Riyadh have drawn no US strike. What has changed is the posture (a third carrier) and the renewed reporting; energy targets were in October's reporting too.
- **The tell:** a US strike on an Iranian refinery, export terminal or power site; or a US official naming energy targets on the record.
