## I. What Happened

### Wall Street had its worst day since April 2025 on AI, not Iran. Oil went the other way, mainly on the ship war (Reuters).
**The crash (Thursday, US).** The S&P 500 fell **4.36%**, its worst day since 4 April 2025, and chip stocks (SOX) **12.25%**, the worst since March 2020. The VIX, Wall Street's fear gauge, jumped from 16.6 to **28.2**. On Friday Korea's KOSPI and Japan's Nikkei each fell 6.0%; the Nikkei and Taiwan are now both more than 20% below their peaks.
- **The cause (NBC, Reuters):** results from Qualcomm and Arm, then a reassessment of what a Democratic Congress means for AI data centres (Sanders: "National data center moratorium now").
- **The war's share:** none found. No strike order, Iranian launch or Israeli strike came in the window.

**Oil (Thursday).** January Brent rose **3.9% to $98.29**, recovering about 40% of Wednesday's fall.
- **The cause (Reuters):** tanker attacks (UKMTO counts at least five incidents since Sunday), Iran's unchanged terms, and Citi's warning that the White House could now escalate. Reuters also cites Ukrainian strikes on Russian refineries; on our estimate about 40% of the rise was those and short-covering after a 16.6% three-day fall.
- **The curve:** only the near month moved; December 2027 rose 15 cents.
- **Friday:** it touched **$100.92** in Asian hours and was ~$98.2 at 16:37 IST.

**What it means.** Oil held its gain while US stocks fell 4.4%, so on Thursday it was not trading risk appetite; most of the rise came before the US open. The VIX spike is not a war signal (see III).

### Trump said a decision is coming; unnamed officials told Fox that 9 November is a new moon. Night three passed with no strike.
**Trump (on camera, Oval Office, late Thursday).** This came after his first Iran meeting with his security team since the midterms and, on the timestamps found (the Times of Israel posted it at 16:54 ET), after Thursday's 14:30 ET oil settle.
- "Now we will make a decision… I would rather not destroy the rest of the country if possible, but maybe I'll have to… You'll know soon enough."
- On the mediators' proposals: "They're not doing anything without our approval."

**Around him (reported).** Reuters' two sources say renewed hostilities are likeliest; one said: "He is planning to bomb Iran. Don't listen to what he's saying." Fox's officials note that **9 November is a new moon**. Axios: CENTCOM is "finalizing" plans with no new directive. The third carrier, *Roosevelt*, will not reach the region before mid-November. OSINT accounts (one origin) report that Iraq will restrict its airspace to designated corridors 05:00–11:00 UTC daily on 7–11 Nov for "active military operations"; no NOTAM or official notice was found.

**The night.** No US or Israeli strike was reported on the third night (English and Persian searches).

**Congress.** Levers were named but no vote held: Democrat Khanna proposes barring funding for combat in Iran; Schumer says many Democratic senators will want Iran concessions before extending funding (Punchbowl); and Republican Massie says he will force a vote to impeach Hegseth in the week of 9 November.

### Iran: a proposal in private, conditions in public.
**The reported proposal.**
- **Reuters:** an unnamed senior Iranian official says Iran sent a **nuclear proposal to Washington via Qatar**, to be discussed only after the US meets Iran's seven demands.
- **NYT:** officials say Tehran is reviewing "concrete proposals".
- **Acknowledgement:** none. Neither Qatar nor the US has acknowledged it on the record.

**On the record.**
- **Mohsen Rezaei** (security council secretary, in Moscow): "As long as they do not execute our seven conditions, we will neither open the Strait of Hormuz nor have negotiations." Iranian state media say he delivered a letter from Mojtaba Khamenei to Putin (the Kremlin confirmed the meeting, not the letter); its contents were not disclosed.
- **Speaker Ghalibaf** answered Vance's election post with mockery: "Why not say this to your boss?" There is still no reply to Vance's letter.

**The channel.** Trump phoned the emir of Qatar on Friday. In the Qatari readout (there is no US one) they discussed "a comprehensive agreement" and the emir urged no further escalation. Brent fell ~$2.4 over the next two hours; Bloomberg says traders "weighed reports of US engagement" in negotiations, though Europe's open and the AI sell-off came in the same window. Foreign minister Araghchi called nine capitals; Oman's readout speaks of "ending the state of war".

**Fire.** There was no Iranian missile or drone salvo at any state (~57 days). Turkey says a soldier was killed on 31 October at a border post by fire "assessed to have come from Iran". That was a border incident, not a strike.

### India closed near its pre-war level, on the largest foreign buying in nearly six years, some of it for a non-war reason.
**The index.** The Nifty rose 2.4% on Thursday and 2.0% on Friday to **25,030.35**. That is its **highest close since 27 February, the last session before the war**, and its best week (+8.6%) since February 2021 (Reuters).

**The money.** Foreign investors bought **₹14,975 crore** on Thursday (exchange provisional). Reuters puts it at about $1.6bn, the largest single day in nearly six years. It was their fifth straight buying day. Domestic funds sold ₹10,445 crore.

**The two engines.**
- **Thursday:** this came before the US crash, in India's first session after Wednesday night's oil fall (PTI's reason). Korea's AI sell-off had already begun: KOSPI −5.3%, foreigners −₩3.42 trillion that day.
- **Friday:** this came after the crash. Brokers named a "reverse AI" rotation out of Korea and Taiwan (Korea saw ₩6.2 trillion of foreign selling over the two days). No fund-level data show it was the same money.

**The rupee.** It went through the dealers' 94.50 cap on two PTI closes: **94.41** on Thursday and **93.89** on Friday (provisional). Reuters calls Friday the strongest since 22 April.
- Flows did most of it: between Thursday's and Friday's rupee closes Brent was about $1 higher, yet the rupee gained 52 paise. But Friday's rally also tracked a ~$4 intraday fall in Brent. HDFC Securities' Parmar calls it "a flow-driven strength", "backed by the supportive mix of softer crude oil, easing yields"; Reuters also cites the post-midterm oil fall.
- No RBI action is named.
- Reserves fell $2.69bn to **$717.06bn** in the week to 30 October, a week that came before these inflows.

**The caution.** The rally was narrow (small caps −0.8%; India VIX +12% to 17.47), and foreigners are still net short index futures. New Delhi has said nothing on the strike window or on Iran's Hormuz "exemption" for Indian ships, which Al Jazeera says comes with transit fees. Pump prices are frozen.

### The ship war: a verified hit north of Qatar with one seafarer killed or missing, and a growing pile of unverified claims.
**The hit.** At 23:20 UTC on 4 November a tanker (UKMTO 196-26) was hit by "two unidentified projectiles" about 50 nautical miles north of Madinat ash Shamal, Qatar. Open-source analysts name it the Greek-operated *Velos Sapphire*.
- **Casualty:** one seafarer was killed (Lloyd's List) or is missing (IMO).
- **Claim:** no one has claimed it.

**The claims.** Iran's forces claimed seven more tankers and a tug, all unnamed and none matched to UKMTO: about 17 since 1 November, with no mine strike verified.

**The threats.** Army spokesman Akraminia: "If Iran is attacked, it will target all assets of the American enemy without regard to any geographical restrictions." An IRGC adviser: "In the event of a new aggression, any country or force that helps America attack Iran can be a legitimate target".

### Israel: Eisenkot 67, Netanyahu 46; Herzog decides on Sunday.
**The count.** Coalition consultations closed with **67 recommendations for Eisenkot and 46 for Netanyahu** (confirmed). Herzog said forming a government is "not entirely clear".

**Liberman.** He dropped his veto on the ultra-Orthodox parties. He kept three lines: end Netanyahu, no new election, and no reliance on the Arab parties. Any 61-seat majority on those terms still needs Shas, which backed Netanyahu.

**US aid.** Netanyahu's office did not deny a US aid cut and called it a move "from aid to partnership"; no State, OMB or Pentagon notice was found.

**Fire.** There was no Israel–Iran fire and no new death in Lebanon.

### Saudi front: Aramco cut Asian prices as futures rose; a Houthi channel reported and half-denied.
**The price cut.** Aramco cut its December Arab Light price for Asia by **$2**, to $7.00 below Oman/Dubai on Argaam's copy of Aramco's table (a $2 cut from November's −$5.00). Newsquawk has $6.50 below, "the lowest since May 2020"; its figure matches Argaam's Extra Light line. On the graph trigger's own definition that is a seller chasing volume, not a scarcity signal, and it fires.

**The air war.** The coalition announced interceptions again: missiles "towards the Madinah region" and "towards Najran", and drones over the Makkah region and the Eastern Province (coalition claims). The Houthis deny targeting Medina and claim a hit on Dammam's airport; Riyadh is silent on the airport claims.

**The channel.** The WSJ reports that Riyadh approached the Houthis, who asked for $51bn plus recognition. Saudi official Rayed Krimly called the demands "pure fabrication"; in the relays found he did not deny the approach. Turkey ratified the Saudi–Turkey–Pakistan pact.
